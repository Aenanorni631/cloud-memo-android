CLOUD MEMO v1.0 - FEATURE LIST
==============================
1. AI ASSISTANT: Floating button access, auto-notebook generation, and idea generation.
2. SYNC: Google Drive (notes/files) and Google Calendar (appointments/notifications).
3. NOTEBOOKS: Titled tabs, PDF/file uploads, and custom notebook colors.
4. TOOLS: Speech-to-text, offline local storage (Room DB), and per-note reminders.
5. WELLNESS: Daily morning affirmations and quotes via notification.
6. UI: Modern/Sleek design. Defaults: Midnight Blue, Black, or White.
